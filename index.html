Frequently Asked Questions

What skill level is required for the snowboarding tours?

We cater to all skill levels! Our instructors offer beginner clinics on gentle slopes as well as advanced backcountry guiding for experienced riders.

Is safety and avalanche equipment provided?

Yes! All high-altitude tours include certified avalanche beacons, probes, shovels, and helmets. Safety orientation is mandatory before departure.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?

You can cancel up to 14 days before your scheduled trip for a full refund. Trips rescheduled due to extreme weather conditions are offered at no extra fee.

What gear should I bring with me?

We recommend bringing thermal base layers, waterproof outerwear, and personal goggles. Boots, boards, and skis are available for rental.
