Meet Lucas Snow

Hey there! I’m Lucas Snow, the founder of Snow Trails Travel Agency. I’ve spent over a decade chasing snowstorms, testing mountain slopes, and riding powder across the globe.

My passion for snowboarding and winter athletics led me to establish this travel agency. My goal is simple: to share the thrill of snow adventures with people who love outdoor exploration just as much as I do.

What Drives Snow Trails?

Unmatched Passion

We don't just book trips — we ride the same slopes and test the same trails to guarantee an authentic experience.

Flexible Packages

Whether you want budget-friendly group tours or premium solo backcountry guides, we tailor options to fit your needs.

Community Focus

Join a vibrant group of fellow winter enthusiasts, swap stories around mountain lodges, and build lifelong friendships.
